# Competitor Landscape

Omni competes in the multi-channel sales engagement and outreach automation space. 

## Key Competitors

### Instantly / Smartlead
- **Strength**: High-volume cold email, unlimited inbox warmup.
- **Omni Advantage**: Omni includes LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and natively integrated AI Voice (Retell), whereas these tools are heavily email-centric. Omni's DAG [[sequence-engine]] supports complex behavioral branching beyond simple email opens/clicks.

### Lemlist
- **Strength**: Multi-channel (Email + LinkedIn) with personalized image generation.
- **Omni Advantage**: True unified nodal canvas allowing absolute visual control over logic gates and branching. Natively supports WhatsApp and AI Voice.

### Apollo.io
- **Strength**: Massive B2B database tightly coupled with an engagement sequencer.
- **Omni Advantage**: Better suited for bespoke, highly customized agency workflows. Omni's architecture allows for "Nested Flows" (e.g., a Retell conversation flow acting as a single node in a larger sequence).

### Clay
- **Strength**: Data enrichment and personalized scraping.
- **Omni Advantage**: Omni focuses on the *dispatch* and *execution* layer. While Clay enriches data, Omni provides the actual "Control Plane" to execute the multi-channel DAG sequences.
