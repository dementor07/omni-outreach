Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 21, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our multi-channel outreach services, including:

  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • Contact information for campaign recipients
  • Campaign performance metrics and analytics
  • Communication logs for compliance and optimization

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Deliver the outreach services you've configured
  • Improve platform performance and reliability
  • Ensure compliance with applicable regulations
  • Provide customer support

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive credentials
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication

4. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with third-party services to deliver functionality. These integrations operate under their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide services or as required by law. Users can request data deletion through their administrator.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Contact your organization's administrator to exercise these rights.

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact your organization's administrator or data protection officer.
